## Datasets available

There are 2 datasets: Weatherbench and uq4turbu

Weatherbench is the main dataset, as it has different resolutions available for the necessary variables. 
Its datasets can be found here: https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/1524895


uq4turbu is just for experimental and analytical purposes. 
Its full dataset can be found here: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/xintongzoucecilie/dataset4turbulence
